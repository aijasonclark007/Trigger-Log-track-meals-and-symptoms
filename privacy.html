Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Trigger Log is operated by triggerlog. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use Trigger Log, why we collect it, and what choices you have. It covers users in the United Kingdom and the United States.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a password, if you create an account. Your password is stored in a protected form by our sign-in provider, and we cannot read it.
  • Google sign-in: if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google.
  • Health information you add: the meals you log, the symptom ratings you give, and any notes you write. This can reveal information about your health, so we treat it as special category data.
  • Guest use: if you don't sign in, your entries are stored only in your browser on that device. We don't receive them.

2. Why we use it

  • To provide the app: saving your check-ins, showing your timeline, and showing patterns in your own log.
  • To sign you in and keep your account secure, including sending confirmation and password reset emails.
  • To respond to questions you send us.

Trigger Log does not sell your information and does not use it for advertising.

3. Our legal basis (UK)

Under the UK GDPR, we rely on your explicit consent to process health information you choose to log. You can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting your check-ins or your account. We rely on the need to run your account and provide the service for everything else.

4. Where your information is stored

Account and check-in data is stored by Supabase in a Stockholm, Sweden data centre, which is in the European Union. The app itself is hosted by Vercel. Both providers process information on our behalf under written data processing terms.

5. Who we share it with

  • Supabase: stores your account and check-ins and runs sign-in.
  • Vercel: hosts the website.
  • Our email provider: sends sign-in and password reset emails.
  • Google: only if you choose to sign in with Google.

We may share information if the law requires it. We do not share health information for marketing.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account and check-ins until you delete them or delete your account. Deleted data is removed from our live systems straight away. Backups are kept for a limited period and then deleted.

7. Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • Access and export: ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Delete: delete all your check-ins in the app using Delete my data, or ask us to delete your account by emailing us.
  • Correct: ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Withdraw consent for processing health information.
  • Complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (UK) or your state attorney general (US).

We will respond within one month in the UK. US state rights vary; we will honour the rights that apply where you live.

8. US state health privacy

Some US states, including Washington, have laws that protect consumer health data. Where those laws apply, we collect health information only with your consent, use it only to provide Trigger Log, and let you request deletion. We don't sell health data.

9. Children

Trigger Log is for adults aged 18 and over. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Medical advice

Trigger Log tracks and shows patterns in your own log. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. It does not replace advice from your doctor or another qualified health professional.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.

12. Contact us
