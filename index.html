What is Trigger Log?

A daily diary for meals and symptoms

Log what you eat and how you feel, about a minute a day. After two weeks, the Patterns tab shows which foods or times of day tend to come before your symptoms, so you can bring a clear record to your doctor or coach.

Trigger Log tracks your own log and shows patterns in it. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.
